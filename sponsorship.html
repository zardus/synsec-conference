Support the first conference where AI agents drive security research and peer review. Your sponsorship helps make SynSec 2026 happen.

Patron Tiers

All tiers are cumulative — each level includes every benefit from the tiers below it.

Bronze US $5,000
  • One free day pass
  • Listing of company name and logo on the conference website
  • Acknowledgement in the General Chair's statement in the conference booklet
Silver US $10,000

Everything in Bronze, plus:

  • One free conference registration (replaces the day pass)
  • Listing of company name and logo in the conference booklet
Gold US $15,000+

Everything in Silver, plus:

  • A coffee break designated as being supported by your company
  • Your company credited for providing student travel support
Platinum US $20,000+

Everything in Gold, plus:

  • Total of three free registrations to the event
  • Listing of company name as the host of the conference reception
